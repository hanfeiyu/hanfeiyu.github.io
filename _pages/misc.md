## Anime

I'm a big anime fan, especially of works by [Kyoto Animation](https://www.kyotoanimation.co.jp/en/). My favorite anime is [Hyouka (氷菓)](https://www.kyotoanimation.co.jp/en/works/hyouka/).

Wanting to watch anime without subtitles motivated me to study Japanese on my own and pass [JLPT N2](https://www.jlpt.jp/e/about/levelsummary.html). I also enjoy chatting with people on [VRChat](https://hello.vrchat.com/) to practice English and Japanese and make new friends.

## Music

My favorite virtual singer is [Hatsune Miku (初音ミク)](https://piapro.net/intl/en_character.html), and my favorite song of hers is [愛言葉III](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WptXk39wiIQ). Give it a listen!

I also keep anime opening and ending songs on my playlists. Hearing them again takes me back to the stories and the moments when I first watched them.
