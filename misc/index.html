Misc

Anime

I’m a big anime fan, especially of works by Kyoto Animation. My favorite anime is Hyouka (氷菓).

Wanting to watch anime without subtitles motivated me to study Japanese on my own and pass JLPT N2. I also enjoy chatting with people on VRChat to practice English and Japanese and make new friends.

Music

My favorite virtual singer is Hatsune Miku (初音ミク), and my favorite song of hers is 愛言葉III. Give it a listen!

I also keep anime opening and ending songs on my playlists. Hearing them again takes me back to the stories and the moments when I first watched them.

Games

I enjoy games across PC, mobile, and consoles. I’ve spent many years playing Dota 2 and StarCraft II. In Dota 2, I usually play mid or safe lane; in StarCraft II, I’m a Terran player (TIMBA!).

Other games I’ve enjoyed on Steam include Terraria, Backpack Battles, Stardew Valley, and many more.

I’m also a fan of the Nintendo Switch and the Pokémon series.
